OUR STORY

Connecting food with purpose.

AaharLink is a food-sharing platform designed to connect food donors with individuals and communities looking for food, helping reduce avoidable food waste.

OUR MISSION

A community where everyone matters.

We aim to make food sharing simple, accessible and organized through technology.

Build connections

OUR APPROACH

How AaharLink makes a difference

Users can register, post food donations, browse food listings and coordinate collection. The platform is designed to support transparency and responsible food sharing.
