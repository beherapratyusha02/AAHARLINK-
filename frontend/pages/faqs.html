Frequently asked questions.

Find answers to common questions about using AaharLink.

What is AaharLink?

AaharLink is a food-sharing platform concept that connects people and organizations with surplus food to people looking for food, with the goal of reducing avoidable food waste.

How can I donate food?

Create an account, log in, open the Donate Food page and provide the food name, category, quantity, location and pickup deadline.

How can I find available food?

Visit the Find Food page to browse food listings. You can search by food name or location and filter by category.

Can I use AaharLink as both a donor and a food seeker?

Yes. During registration, select the Both option to indicate that you want to donate food and find food.

Is the food free?

AaharLink is designed to facilitate food donations. The terms of each listing should be clear, and users should confirm collection details with the donor.

How do I know whether food is safe?

Donors should provide accurate preparation, storage and pickup information. Food seekers should confirm these details and follow appropriate food-safety practices before accepting food.

Can I edit or remove my food listing?

Editing and removal are planned features. They need to be implemented with the backend and database before they are available in the live platform.

Is AaharLink available in every city?

The platform can be developed for multiple locations. Actual availability depends on the food donations and community participation in each area.
